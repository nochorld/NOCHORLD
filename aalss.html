Synopsis
Todd, an unlucky playboy, accidentally rescues the heiress of a massive corporate empire. In return, he receives a high-paying job and a full college sponsorship. However, this sudden fortune plunges him into a world of dark corporate secrets and impending revenge that threatens his new life and romance.
